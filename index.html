03 Games for the room

Turn the draw
into a show.

Two games that pick a winner while everyone watches. Every round is drawn at random, so everyone has the same chance.

  • Race: 2–6 players drive to the finish line, with surprise super steps
  • Last one standing: names drop out round by round, down to a final two
  • Play on its own or one round per tap
  • Turn your phone on its side to fill a TV
