01 Your tickets

Works with the tickets you already sold.

You don't type in every ticket. You enter the ranges you printed or bought, the way they're numbered on paper.

  • Numbered tickets

    A roll or sheet: 1–100, 1–1000, or with leading zeros like 001.

  • Numbered booklets

    Books numbered straight through: 1–100, 101–200 and on.

  • Lettered booklets

    One letter per book, each from 1: A1–100, B1–100.

  • Color series

    Colored rolls, each from 1: Red 1–100, Blue 1–100.

How to draw raffle tickets
with RafflePilot.

1

Enter your tickets

Pick the ticket type and type the ranges, for example A1–100 and B1–100. Two books of 100 take a few seconds, not 200 entries.

2

Leave out unsold tickets

Type the numbers nobody bought, like 12, 87 or 120–150. Only sold tickets are in the draw.

3

Reveal the winner

Tap Draw. The number spins, the winner lands on a big golden ticket, and nobody can win twice.

02 On the night

Made for a room
full of people.

Hold up the phone, or turn it on its side and connect it to a TV or projector so everyone can follow the draw.

  • One winner per prize, in the order you choose
  • Several winners in one draw when you have many prizes
  • Undo the last draw if a winner isn't in the room
  • A history of every winner, ready to share with your group

Questions
organizers ask.

Is the draw fair?

Every remaining ticket has the same chance. Winners are picked with your phone's cryptographic random number generator, and a ticket that has won can't be drawn again.

Does it need internet?

No. RafflePilot works fully offline, so it works in a sports hall or a basement without signal. There's no account to create.

Is it free?

You can try it for free with numbered tickets, up to 20 tickets in one raffle. Ticket books, color series and bigger raffles are in the full version, a one-time purchase with no subscription.

Does it sell tickets?

No. You sell your tickets the way you always have. RafflePilot only draws the winners and doesn't handle money.

RafflePilot also calls 30-, 75-, 80- and 90-ball bingo, draws from a list of names, and runs prize draws. See what's free and what's in the full version.
